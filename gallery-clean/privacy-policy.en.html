Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Yintang Gallery Clean ("we", "the app") values your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information. The app is a local photo cleanup tool: media stays on your device.

1. Information we process

  • On-device media: photos and videos are scanned and, only after you confirm, deleted on your device. Media files are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Device information: basic technical data needed to run the app (e.g. OS version, crash diagnostics).
  • Advertising identifiers: when ads are shown, Google AdMob may use advertising IDs and related signals as described in Google's policies.

2. How we use information

  • To find duplicates, large files, screenshots, and similar photos on your device;
  • To delete items you explicitly confirm;
  • To show ads that support the free app;
  • To improve stability via crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics).

3. Ads

This app shows ads through Google AdMob (interstitial and rewarded formats). Ad requests may include device and advertising identifiers. Google may process this data according to the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Core cleanup features remain available if an ad fails to load;
  • Deep scan requires watching a rewarded ad to unlock;
  • Where required by law (e.g. EEA/UK), Google may present a consent / privacy options form.

4. Crash reporting

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash and non-fatal error reports so we can fix bugs. Reports may include device model, OS version, app version, and stack traces. They are not used to identify your photos.

5. Information sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not operate a media-upload backend for this app. Data may be processed by service providers that power ads and crash reporting (Google).

6. Data security

Media processing is local. Network features (ads, crash reports) use encrypted transport where provided by those SDKs. Please keep your device secure.

7. Your choices

  • You can revoke photo/media permission in system settings;
  • You can uninstall the app to stop all local processing;
  • You may reset advertising identifiers in system settings;
  • Contact us for privacy questions at zepar05041@gmail.com.

8. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). It contains ads and is intended for a general adult audience.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy and publish changes in the app or on this page. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
